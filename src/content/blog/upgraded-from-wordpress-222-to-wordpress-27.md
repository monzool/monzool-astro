**WORDPRESS 2.7 UPGRADE** from WordPress 2.2.2 done with no problems. I was somewhat worried that my old theme would break on the upgrade, but It appears to be working. Well, I got at little issue with the Wp-Syntax plugin. I upgraded the plugin and now my GeShi style overrides do not work anymore - guess that is things one discover when doing infrequent upgrading.

I'm looking forward to working with this new WordPress version and exploring its added features.
