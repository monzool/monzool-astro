**I FOLDED A** thousand paper cranes. [Japanese legend](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thousand_origami_cranes) believes the cranes will grant you a wish. The daughter of a couple of friends of mine, were up for [confirmation](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confirmation), and so a gift of _long life and good health_ seemed like a good idea `;-)`
