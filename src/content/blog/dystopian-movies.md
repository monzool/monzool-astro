**I STUMBLED UPON** an excellent [Top 50 Dystopian Movies of All Time](http://snarkerati.com/movie-news/the-top-50-dystopian-movies-of-all-time/ "Top 50 Dystopian Movies of All Time") ranking list. Even though my personal views of technology and the future are definitely more [utopian](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utopia "Utopia"), I've always found these [dystopian](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dystopia) movies to be the most fascinating. Interestingly this list does in fact list include most of my favorite movies, even though I do not agree with the ranking order in which they are listed.

My personal top 10 dystopian movie hit list would go like this:

- 10\. [The City of Lost Children](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0112682/)
- 9\. [Brazil](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0088846/)
- 8\. [RoboCop](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0093870/)
- 7\. [Akira](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0094625/)
- 6\. [12 Monkeys](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0114746/)
- 5\. [Dark City](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0118929/)
- 4\. [Gattaca](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0119177/)
- 3\. [Matrix](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0133093/)
- 2\. [Alien](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0078748/)
- 1\. [Blade Runner](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0083658/)
