**DYSTOPIAN MOVIES ARE** a favorite genre of mine. In 2007 I made a [list](https://monzool.net/blog/2007/09/29/dystopian-movies/) of my ten favorite movies in the category, and after revisiting that blog post, I thought now would be a good time to revise the list

With no further ado, this is my new list:

- 20. [28 Days Later (2002)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0289043)
- 19. [Equilibrium (2002)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0238380)
- 18. [The City of Lost Children (1998)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0112682)
- 17. [Pitch Black (2000)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0134847)
- 16. [Chappie (2015)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1823672)
- 15. [Brazil (1985)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0088846/)
- 14. [Children of Men (2006)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0206634)
- 13. [V for Vendetta (2005)](#%20V%20for%20Vendetta)
- 12. [Elysium (2013)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1535108/)
- 11. [RoboCop (1987)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0093870/)
- 10. [Logan (2017)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3315342)
- 9. [Akira (1988)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0094625/)
- 8. [Gattaca (1997)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0119177/)
- 7. [District 9 (2009)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1136608)
- 6. [The Creator (2023)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt11858890/)
- 5. [The Terminator (1984)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0088247)
- 4. [Dark City (1998)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0118929/)
- 3. [Blade Runner (1982)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0083658/)
- 2. [Dredd (2012)](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1343727)
- 1. [Alien (1979)](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0078748/)

The Matrix went out. I think I just saw that movie too many times 🎃 Otherwise the majority of the top of the list is probably unsurprising for most people (including myself).

Movies like _Alien_ and _Blade Runner_ are just timeless classics that is as good today, as they were then. Same goes for classics like _The Terminator_ and _RoboCop_ - although they perhaps show there age a bit more.

I placed _Dredd_ at second best. I just absolutely love that movie. Its brutal, claustrophobic, have an bad-ass anti-hero and is absolutely dystopian.

It is obvious that most of the movies are rather old by now. I made the original list in 2007, and only six movies created since the first list, made it to the list! But its a list of all good movies, and it makes me want to see them all again. It’s been a really really long time since I saw _Dark City_, _Gattaca_ and _Brazil_. I think I’ll start there - they are perfect for a dark and rainy winter night 📽️
